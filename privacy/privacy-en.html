The Service requires no account registration and does not collect directly identifying personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address. However, to improve quality and analyze usage, the following information may be collected automatically:

The Service uses Google Analytics (via Google Tag Manager), provided by Google LLC, to analyze usage. The information above may be transmitted to and processed by Google. You can learn more about Google's data practices below:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Retention

Collected analytics data is deleted once its purpose has been fulfilled, and is automatically removed after a period in accordance with Google Analytics' data retention policy.

5. Your Rights

You may opt out of analytics collection by changing your device's advertising/tracking settings or by using the Google Analytics opt-out tool above. You may also contact us at the address below to request access to or deletion of your information.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 14 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.
